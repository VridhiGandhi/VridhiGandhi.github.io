My Approach

How Every Engagement Works

01
Understand the System
Before writing code, I map the full problem — what's upstream, what's downstream, what breaks if I touch the wrong thing. Most solutions fail because this step was skipped.
02
Design to Last
Quick fixes create future work. I design for the scale you're heading to, not just the problem you have today — clean architecture, documented decisions, testable outputs.
03
Build and Validate
I build in stages with validation at every step. Pipelines get tested against real data. APIs get verified end-to-end. Systems get load-tested before they go live.
04
Ship with Confidence
Delivery includes documentation, monitoring hooks, and a handoff that makes sense. I don't disappear after launch — I make sure the solution actually holds up in production.
