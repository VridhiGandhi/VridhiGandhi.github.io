My Story

How I Got Here

As a kid I was obsessed with how things connected — patterns in games, logic in math, systems hidden inside everyday things. That instinct didn't go away. It became my career.

I studied Computer Engineering with an AI specialization and graduated with Honours in Data Science & Analysis (A+) from NMIMS. But my real education happened in the messy, real-world spaces where nothing is clean and everything is interconnected.

Every role I've taken — from streaming millions of events at Merkle, to automating 10,000 devices at Streambox, to building the backend and recommendation engine at Lionsgate — has been about the same thing: bridging technical complexity into working solutions.

That's what Solution Engineering is. I just didn't have the title for it until recently.

How I Work

What I Actually Believe

Systems Over Symptoms
I don't patch what I don't understand. Before writing a line of code, I want to know why the problem exists, where it lives in the system, and what breaks if I fix it the wrong way. That instinct is what makes the difference between a band-aid and a real solution.
Precision over Speed
I work fast. But I never rush what matters. When something is running at scale — streaming pipelines, OTT infrastructure, automated device fleets — a careless shortcut becomes a production incident at 2 AM. I build things that hold up.
Bridge the Gap
The most valuable work happens at the seam between systems — where data meets product, where backend meets user, where engineering meets business logic. I'm most useful right there, translating between layers that don't naturally talk to each other.
Learn to Stay Current
From Kafka to GraphQL to Veo — the stack shifts constantly. I don't hold onto tools I've outgrown or avoid ones I haven't touched yet. The skill isn't knowing everything. It's knowing how to learn fast, apply well, and move on when something better exists.
